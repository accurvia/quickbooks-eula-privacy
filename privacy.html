Privacy Policy This page describes how we collect, use, and protect data when using the QuickBooks integration. What data is collected Read-only access to QuickBooks data via OAuth 2.0 (vendors, AP/bills, invoices, payments, etc.). No passwords or sensitive credentials are stored in this app. How data is used Data is used solely to answer questions about your QuickBooks data through this integration. It is not sold or shared with third parties. Data retention Data is kept as long as the integration is active unless you request deletion. Security Access is read-only and secured via OAuth 2.0. Tokens and secrets are stored securely on your server (not in this chat). Your rights You can revoke access at any time from QuickBooks and remove the pages by deleting the repository.
